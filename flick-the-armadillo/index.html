Flick the Armadillo

Flick a rolled-up armadillo up through a world full of spikes. One finger, one flick at a time. How far can you climb?

Coming soon to Google Play

How to play

👆

Flick anywhere

Drag and release anywhere on the screen. Your finger never covers the armadillo.

⏱️

Time it right

You can’t flick while the armadillo is still rising. Wait for the moment, then go.

💀

Don’t touch the spikes

Spikes above, spikes below, and spikes chasing you up from the bottom of the screen.

📏

Climb the lines

Every line you pass is a point. The higher you climb, the faster the spikes come.

🏆

Beat your best

Chase today’s best and your all-time record, one more go at a time.

📣

Show off

Share your best score of the day with your friends and dare them to beat it.
